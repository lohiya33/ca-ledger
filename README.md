# CA Final — Study Ledger & Mock Test Command Center

A high-performance, offline-first study ledger designed specifically for CA Final aspirants.

## Features
- **Master Command Dashboard**: Readiness percentage, days countdown, study streak, and daily study goal ring.
- **Stage Dashboards**: Track First Study (FS), Revision 1 (R1), Revision 2 (R2), and Revision 3 (R3) for every chapter.
- **Single-Subject Tests & Mock Hub**:
  - Log tests by chapter, partial syllabus, or full mock.
  - Interactive SVG score trend chart with ICAI benchmarks (40% Pass, 50% Aggregate, 60% Exemption).
  - Chapter coverage matrix sorting "Never Tested" chapters to top.
  - Centralized pre-exam mistake notebook.
- **1.5-Day Last Day Revision (LDR) Sheet**:
  - Star key chapters to generate a clean 36-hour pre-exam revision cheat sheet.
  - Record must-revise question numbers and personal remarks.
  - Automatically imports mistake takeaways from past tests.
- **Study Log with Stopwatch & Pomodoro Timer**:
  - 50-minute Pomodoro focus timer or Stopwatch with subject tagging.
  - One-click session logging.
- **Spaced Repetition Warning**: Flags chapters overdue (>21 days) for revision.
- **Theme & Printing**: Midnight Dark Mode toggle and clean, borderless print-to-PDF formatting.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable on Android, iOS, and Desktop with 100% offline functionality.
- **Google Drive Cloud Sync**: Seamless, private cross-device sync between phone and PC.
